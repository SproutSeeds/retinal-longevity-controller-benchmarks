1. Retinal longevity and the recovery measurement

We want to understand whether a reprogramming response can preserve adult cell identity and recover after the factors are withdrawn. I have expanded the public-data analysis, corrected assumptions against the source methods, and packaged the remaining measurement gap. The current work supports a more precise research plan. It does not establish a retinal therapy.

2. Four questions

The benchmark separates late identity, unusual states, recovery and continuity. The inspected public sources give us pieces of those answers across different experiments. They do not yet provide the matched late human trajectory needed to resolve all four. Combining unrelated files cannot create that missing observation.

3. Public-data foundation

The source teams have already generated valuable observations. Our contribution is to make those observations usable under explicit rules, test what the analyses actually show, and preserve the limits. Human fibroblasts, mouse liver and retinal pigment epithelium remain separate biological systems. The source audit includes ten priority resources and the specific missing inputs for each.

4. Guide correction

Recovering the supplementary methods changed a key assumption. The vectors contain two targeting guides, so our earlier single-guide dominance rule discarded intended paired-guide cells. The revised primary rule produces 208 eligible comparisons, compared with 97 under our earlier rule. This increases usable comparisons, not independent donors. The EZH2 signal moves close to zero, and the E2F3 expression-direction check fails. Exact replication still needs the original assignment table and control-vector pair map.

5. Ocular endpoint

The mouse RPE correlation stays positive across the declared sensitivities. The recovered full text clarifies that the authors used a fitted clock with different preprocessing. Our broad expression correlation is a different endpoint, so it cannot establish whether their clock result replicates. We need the historical fitted model, reference inputs and exact animal and cohort mapping. These expression matrices also do not independently reproduce vision outcomes.

6. Treatment-recognition model

The fixed-panel model separates SRSF1 from its control in each of three held-out donors. Its observed AUROC is one, while depth and detected genes alone reach about 0.83. There are only three donors and repeated libraries, with experimental batch unrecorded. The result is a treatment-recognition diagnostic. It does not quantify rejuvenation, and it does not justify scaling into a large intervention model without a compatible independent study.

7. Synthetic sampling

These are synthetic sampling scenarios, kept separate from biological data. At the same total of 1,200 cells, the chance of detecting at least one true state rises from about 53 percent with one donor to 81 percent with three and 92 percent with six under the displayed assumptions. The assumptions are hypothetical. The exercise shows why cell count alone is an incomplete sampling justification and why detector sensitivity and specificity both need calibration.

8. Observed withdrawal comparator

The mouse liver archive supplies an actual observed factor-off endpoint. The deposited files have 16,042 nonempty barcodes. Our post hoc interpretation of the paper’s count and mitochondrial filters retains 2,288, close to the reported 2,284. The exact four-cell discrepancy remains unresolved. This is a useful observed comparator and software fixture, with a different species, tissue and recovery duration from the human question.

9. Conditional pilot

The proposed focused pilot retains untreated references, continued exposure and withdrawal. A shared baseline and a common induced condition before a proposed day-17 branch give eleven biological collections per donor. With three donors that is 33 single-cell libraries. Three donors are a feasibility starting point, not a power calculation. The intervention, branch timing, assay calibration, culture replicates and donor requirements need review by a qualified experimental partner before execution.

10. Funding sequence

The first request is for reusable metadata and compatible existing material. If the joint measurement gap remains, the focused pilot would estimate feasibility and donor variation. Adding four earlier timepoints with reference and induced conditions brings the full ladder to 57 libraries. Longer follow-up is a separate extension. A retinal or RPE functional bridge is also a distinct study. The package contains budget quantities and deliverables; actual quotes, a named partner and appropriate review are still required. No wet-lab work has been purchased or arranged.

11. Public review and next evidence

The public repository includes executable analysis, source hashes, numerical results, model limitations, synthetic assumptions and the conditional measurement plan. The 75 checks and twelve failure-case tests establish the stated computational properties. The four biological readouts remain open. A contributor can change that decision by supplying the exact missing observation, resolving source metadata or demonstrating a reproducible error. The next funding discussion can therefore focus on the measurement that would materially advance the project.
