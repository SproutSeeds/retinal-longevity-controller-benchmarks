1. Nine paired neural donors

I compared treated and untreated cultures from nine independent mouse donors at the only timepoint with complete age and treatment groups. The cell-cycle panel decreased in every donor under all three scoring methods, and every single-gene omission retained that direction. The secondary sign-test Holm value is 0.015625. The primary CPM age contrasts pass the four-panel Holm rule, while rank scoring weakens them and reverses the stress contrast. These are acute transcriptional responses. They do not identify rejuvenation, functional arrest or a causal effect of age. The well layout differs across groups, and its relation to preparation order is unknown.

2. Animal sensitivity across cohorts

The mouse tissue analysis gives a negative mean microglial cell-cycle score in both cohorts. Cohort two retains a negative sign under animal omission, although one omission falls below the descriptive effect floor. Cohort one has a smaller mean and changes sign when either of two influential animals is omitted. Agreement between means therefore falls short of robust agreement across animals. The cohorts come from the same study and differ in age, sex composition and sequencing chemistry. The complete report retains every eligible cell type and all failed support checks. This comparison is separate from the nine-donor cultured neural result.

3. Human marker concentration

The original human bulk arithmetic reproduces. The interpretation needed a narrower description. CD24 contributes about eighty to ninety percent of the signed seven-gene panel difference, depending on construct and normalization. Removing CD24 preserves the group mean sign, but individual library comparisons can reverse. The raw matrix also distinguishes absent features from genes present with sparse or zero counts. Low RNA detection does not establish that a biological network is off. I preserved the earlier numerical files and added this correction with every gene contribution.

4. Joint human marker observations

I separately measured endogenous-marker co-detection and fibroblast-marker expression in two donors over four timepoints. The primary joint identity-low and co-detection fractions at day seven are 11.74 percent and 22.01 percent. The table shows co-detection alone and its measurement sensitivity. Requiring two UMIs per marker reduces the observed fractions. Equalizing eligible cells to one thousand UMIs reduces them further and changes the retained denominator. Three seeded thinning repetitions and every predefined sensitivity remain in the report. These are different cells at each timepoint, with one donor at each age. They do not provide a true rare-state prevalence, an age-effect estimate or controlled withdrawal evidence.

5. Conditional composition decomposition

I separated the conditional per-cell average into composition and within-type terms under a frozen symmetric formula. In the stress panel those terms oppose each other. The primary support includes Microglia and Oligodendrocyte and retains roughly fifty-nine to sixty-eight percent of each animal’s recovered cells. This exact identity concerns a different summary from pseudobulk. It does not identify causal tissue-composition changes. Named-type mixtures, detection depth and selection into support remain relevant. The public extension reports both one-sided conventions, gene contributions and a separate depth sensitivity.

6. Existing-data recovery and the next decision

The most concrete recovery target is a complete barcode-to-animal map or the prefilter objects for seven already-sequenced samples. Five have a withdrawal label, but exact removal and harvest timing still need source confirmation. The current raw inventory does not provide verified assignments for them. Recovering those observations could improve the mouse comparison without commissioning another experiment. It would still leave the matched late human and ocular measurement to establish. The public funding plan already describes a conditional feasibility collection and the full timing design. Synthetic data can test software, detection assumptions and sampling choices. They cannot supply the missing biological outcome. No wet-lab operations were purchased or arranged.
