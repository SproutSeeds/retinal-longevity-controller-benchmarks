# Source attribution

The original experiments and model belong to their researchers. This package contributes extraction, provenance checks, sample joins, intake validation and bounded interpretation.

- Plesa and colleagues, [Transcriptomic reprogramming screen identifies SRSF1 as a regulator of aging](https://www.nature.com/articles/s43587-026-01234-8), Supplementary Table 1: the numerical model terms extracted in `srsf1-clock-parameters.json`. These are source-derived factual parameters, not a new trained model. Original article and supplement terms remain applicable; this project's MIT license does not relicense third-party material.
- [GSE325735](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE325735): human count-gene coverage and withdrawal sample annotations. The raw matrix is downloaded separately; no donor identities are inferred from pooled or technical replicates.
- [saschajung/Clock](https://github.com/saschajung/Clock/tree/7ae5f38b7ea74f76cb13076b64350d5930bb152f): pinned code/history inspection. No author code is included or executed.
- Lu and colleagues, [ocular preprint](https://doi.org/10.1101/2025.08.30.673239): source interpretation and public GEO sample annotations. Full text is not redistributed.
- [Gladyshev-Lab/tAge](https://github.com/Gladyshev-Lab/tAge), pinned revision `d048ba6016a7e5a114f45195e0b11071e2b8c424`, and the identified Zenodo records: model inventory only. The source states non-commercial, non-revenue-generating academic-use terms. The clocks have not been downloaded, executed or redistributed in this package.
- Krieger and colleagues, [GSE350289](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE350289): metadata completeness check. Large expression matrices were not downloaded.

The three retrieval receipts identify exact public URLs, checked times, byte counts, SHA-256 hashes and access failures. Copyrighted papers, source spreadsheets, author scripts, contact drafts and local cache paths are excluded from publication.
