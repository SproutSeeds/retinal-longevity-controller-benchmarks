**October 18, 2026 clarification:** historical tAge replication remains pending because the required inputs are unconfirmed. No tAge or TACO model has been run here. There is no failed independent clock replication and no competing estimate of the reported GSTA4 age reduction.

Earlier dated artifacts use the phrase "not independently reproduced here." That phrase meant the calculation had not been completed. It should not be read as a completed attempt that contradicted the authors. Current reader-facing summaries now state the pending status explicitly; the underlying numerical records and source hashes are preserved.

The completed expression comparison uses the OSK libraries and compares old-minus-young with OSK-minus-vector changes. Its r = +0.663 across 13,327 genes is a gene-wise expression statistic. The paper's reported 8.5-month tAge reduction concerns GSTA4 and a fitted transcriptomic clock. These are separate intervention contrasts and separate endpoints.

We located later tAge archives. Their existence does not establish which model, imputation values and control reference produced the historical predictions. An authoritative identification of those assets and a small input/output example can resolve that dependency. Animal, eye and cohort joins are additionally needed for interpreting comparisons with functional measurements.

The [October 18 decision audit](../2026-10-decision-audit/README.md) records the completed public-source work. [Lu et al.'s original preprint](https://doi.org/10.1101/2025.08.30.673239) remains the source of the biological findings. The [earlier public revision](https://github.com/SproutSeeds/retinal-longevity-controller-benchmarks/tree/7238288b610d7ca75c1b045c03fdfb8f4b99f116) preserves the full dated record before this clarification.

This wording update changes no numerical result, treatment conclusion or assessment of experimental replacement. The planned collaboration remains a proposal; a scientific partnership has not been established by this packet.
