Raw third-party matrices, RDS objects, full metadata responses and author code stay in a temporary source cache and are obtained directly from their repositories for reproduction. The repository's software license does not relicense third-party data or publications. Reusers should retain the source attribution and applicable source terms. Human sample labels here are already-public research identifiers; no private correspondence is included.
