Public-data reuse remains the first route. Inspect compatible existing deposits and banked material before proposing new collection. This pass has not shown that a new experiment is avoidable, and it has not established that every usable public source has been exhausted.

The existing [conditional measurement plan](https://github.com/SproutSeeds/retinal-longevity-controller-benchmarks/blob/main/research/2026-10-public-data/MEASUREMENT_PLAN.md) remains the funding basis if the joint gap survives: 33 biological collections for the proposed three-donor feasibility pilot, or 57 for the full nine-timepoint design. These quantities are conditional planning arithmetic, not a validated sample-size recommendation. New simulation results strengthen the need to estimate donor variation and detector specificity before setting a confirmatory design. No wet-lab purchase, booking or arrangement has occurred.

A larger custom model should wait for a defined observed target and compatible independent donors/study evaluation, including depth, cycle and batch baselines. The one-donor OSK comparator cannot serve as an independent SRSF1 classifier test. Synthetic data can stress-test analysis and sampling assumptions; they cannot fill an unmeasured biological endpoint.
