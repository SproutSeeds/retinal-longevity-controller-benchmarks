# Source attribution and reuse

Our evaluator code, derived analysis code and authored explanations use the repository's MIT license. Original studies and data retain their own authorship and terms. The MIT license does not relicense third-party workbooks, publications, datasets or models.

| Source | Original authority | Exported material and scope |
|---|---|---|
| GSE132828 | [GEO record](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE132828), source investigators | Derived condition-level RNA scores, contrasts and uncertainty summaries. Original raw matrices and supplements are not redistributed here. |
| GSE126633 | [GEO record](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE126633), source investigators | Derived adult-primary donor-line RNA scores and contrasts; excluded patient samples remain labeled. |
| GSE159435 | [Butler et al.](https://doi.org/10.1111/jcmm.16569), [GEO](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE159435) | Derived adult donor age-model summaries, with source-defined panels. |
| GSE297557 and functional source data | [Piskova et al.](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-026-71493-x), [GEO](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE297557) | Derived impairment contrasts and selected numerical functional observations, retaining worksheet/range attribution. The manuscript and complete source workbook are not redistributed. |
| GSE90889 | [GEO record](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE90889), source investigators | Derived paired NAM expression summaries on the authors' normalized scale. No blanket CC BY assertion is made for the source workbook or manuscript. |
| GSE224438 | [Xu, Brunet and colleagues](https://doi.org/10.1038/s43587-024-00594-3), [GEO](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE224438) | Derived animal-level panel summaries. The additional retained-cell objects were shared with CC BY 4.0 reuse permission. This release credits the investigators and contains transformed summaries, rather than the shared objects or private delivery links. |

Please cite the original study for each biological dataset and this repository's release commit for the derived analysis and evaluator. Raw inputs should be obtained from their authoritative public source or through the investigators under the applicable terms. Author-only clock outputs, historical model artifacts and correspondence are excluded from this release.
