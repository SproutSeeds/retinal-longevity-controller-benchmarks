# Linked RNA and measured-function input audit

Level: Verified for deterministic source extraction and admission replay. Acquisition rank and biological interpretation: Heuristic. Joint functional inference and restoration: INCONCLUSIVE.

The canonical artifacts in this folder bind an eight-study source review, 177 recovered RNA metadata records, 13 source-labeled within-RNA contrasts across three Gill donor labels, and ten Schiff functional-line summaries with 26 reported experiments. The reviewed candidate roster admits zero joint RNA/function comparisons under the frozen contract.

Verification hook: `python3 verify.py` and `python3 audit_sources.py --verify`. Expected: PASS for portable replay and retained-source extraction, respectively. See VERIFICATION_RECORD.md for dependencies, source coverage and determinism. The source-specific acquisition failures remain in FAILED_PATHS.json.

The audit supplies a ranked intake table, exact RNA-side identifiers and a next-input specification. It does not calculate expression effects, attach functional values to unverified RNA units, identify a safe intervention, establish rejuvenation or advance Phase 483. Temporary identity transitions, pluripotent-derived systems and primary adult cells keep separate scopes. Original investigators retain credit for their biological findings.

The gate is a deterministic check on explicit reviewed inputs, not independent scientific adjudication. The synthetic acceptance control is isolated in test_admission.py and excluded from all observed candidate counts.
